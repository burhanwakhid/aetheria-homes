Two Ways to Transform

Whether you need a single facelift or a floor-to-roof rebuild, our renovation team scopes the work around your budget and your daily life.

Our Pricing Philosophy

Every House Is Unique. So Is Every Quote.

Unlike a new build on empty land, a renovation always meets an existing structure — hidden wiring, uneven foundations, and site quirks that no generic price-per-square-meter list can honestly account for. That's why we don't publish flat renovation rates.

Instead, our surveyors visit your property, assess the real condition of your space, and translate every finding into a transparent, itemized RAB (Rencana Anggaran Biaya / Bill of Quantities) — so you know exactly what you're paying for before a single wall comes down.

  • Full Material Specifications

    Every brick, tile, and finish is listed by brand, quantity, and unit price — no vague allowances.

  • Labor Cost Breakdown

    Clear labor charges per work stage, from demolition to final finishing touches.

  • Realistic Work Timeline

    A stage-by-stage schedule so you always know what's happening and when.

  • No Hidden Costs Guarantee

    The RAB is the final number — any change is discussed and approved with you first.
